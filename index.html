01 / The practice

Design that connects people, place and possibility.

Metako Projex is an architectural practice serving residential, commercial and public-sector projects, with a building-construction division focused on medium-sized developments.

A considered approach

From first idea to built environment.

Founded in 2004, Metako Projex brings architectural thinking and construction capability together. Its published work spans private residences, commercial environments and public-sector projects.
